Disclaimer

Please read this carefully before using these calculators.

These calculators use the Effective Monthly Rate formula: monthly rate = (1 + annual rate)^(1/12) – 1. This is the same methodology used by Franklin Templeton India and other leading fund houses.

  • Mutual Fund investments are subject to market risks. Past performance is not indicative of future results.
  • The SIP calculator uses the annuity-due formula (investment at start of each period) with effective monthly compounding.
  • The Lumpsum calculator uses annual compounding: FV = PV × (1 + r)^n.
  • The SWP calculator simulates month-by-month corpus depletion using the effective monthly rate.
  • The STP calculator simulates source fund depletion and target fund accumulation simultaneously.
  • Results do not account for expense ratios, exit loads, taxes (LTCG/STCG), or inflation unless specifically mentioned.
  • SEBI Registration: IPFS (Invest and Prosper Financial Services), ARN: 24216.

These projections are illustrative only. Please consult a SEBI-registered financial advisor before making investment decisions.
